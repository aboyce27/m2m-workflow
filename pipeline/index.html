M2M Program Playbook
Migrate to Mailchimp · Service Partner Team · Snapshot: · 13 records across 6 pipeline stages
This playbook documents the full operational logic of the M2M Customer Intake Pipeline — how deals flow, what every flag means, where current bottlenecks are, and how to read this dashboard. It's built so anyone picking this up can get oriented fast without digging through the underlying spreadsheet.
What This Program Does
Migrate to Mailchimp (M2M) is a managed migration service pairing Mailchimp partner agencies with customers who need help moving from another ESP. The Service Partner Team owns end-to-end delivery: intake validation, partner sourcing, SOW approval, migration execution, and close-out. Every card in the pipeline represents one customer engagement. Revenue is tracked as MRR — 3x net MRR is the standard approved funding amount.
The 7 Pipeline Stages
1 · Intake & Validation
AE or PDM submits intake form. Team validates eligibility (Premium plan, consent, service fit). Eligibility decision sent within 48 hrs.
2 · Partner Sourcing
Team identifies best-fit partner by vertical, ESP, list size, geo. Capacity check sent. Status: “Partner Capacity Check Sent.”
3 · Partner Confirmed
Partner confirms availability. Assignment formalized. Partner introduced to customer. Usually fastest stage — 48hr response typical.
4 · Discovery & SOW
Partner runs discovery call. SOW drafted, approved, submitted to Coupa. Current bottleneck — 4 of 10 active deals stuck here waiting for PO.
5 · Active Migration
PO issued. Kickoff held. Partner executes migration. Weekly status reports required. Customer check-ins at set intervals.
6 · Delivery & Close
Partner submits Proof of Delivery. PoD approved. CSAT survey sent. Success comm sent. Project closed and revenue recorded.
7 · Closed / Lost
Customer withdrew or deal fell through. Current cycle losses: USCJ (CiviCRM integration not viable), Window World (customer withdrew), Scoria World (timing).

Core Bottleneck: SOW to Coupa
4 of 10 active deals are at “Deal Closed — Sourcing Pending” — Stone Brewing, Streamsong, Medical Solutions, and Humanitarian Data Solutions. All have a confirmed partner, a signed customer, and an approved deal. The stall is the per-migration SOW-to-Coupa PO requirement. This is the exact friction Phase 2 of the M2M program is designed to eliminate through a program-level non-PO payment model.
Capacity Check Limbo (70–90 Days)
Southern Spirits, Body Design, and It’s Only Natural have been in “Partner Capacity Check Sent” for 2–3 months with no logged response. Needs an immediate status call: either confirm or close.
Active Migration: Overdue on Status Reports
Fittes (Klaviyo → Mailchimp, Canada) is the only deal in active migration. Weekly status reports show OVERDUE. These are a contract requirement and a PoD dependency — needs a check-in with Ranjana Kaur now.
Lost Deal Patterns
Three losses, three causes: USCJ needed CiviCRM integration Mailchimp can’t support (product gap); Window World customer withdrew (likely cost/timeline); Scoria World ran out of time before their Klaviyo campaign deadline (timing mismatch). None are process failures — they’re fit and timing mismatches worth tracking as loss-reason taxonomy grows.
How to Use This Dashboard
Pipeline Board — daily view. Red left-border = overdue. Amber = at-risk. Click any card for full deal detail including notes and Salesforce link.
Action Queue — use for daily standup or weekly sync. Sorted by urgency. Shows partner and program deliverables side by side.
All Deals — flat table view. Click any row for detail.
